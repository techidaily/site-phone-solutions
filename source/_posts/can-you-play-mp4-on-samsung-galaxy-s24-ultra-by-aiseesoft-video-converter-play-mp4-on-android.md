  MP4 is among Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra supported video formats, however, some users still encounter incompatibility issues when playing MP4 files on Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra. Why does this happen? The reason is that MP4 is a container format which may contain multiple video and audio streams encoded with various codecs. Although Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra is compatible with MP4 video files, it also requires the MP4 file to be encoded in the way that is recognized by your particular device model. Some MP4 videos will not play on Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra due to the failure of codec recognition.
