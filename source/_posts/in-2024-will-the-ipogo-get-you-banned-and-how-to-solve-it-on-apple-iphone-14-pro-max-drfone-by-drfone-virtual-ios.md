"Location Not Available" means that the Apple iPhone 14 Pro Max device is not currently sharing its location with the Find My app. This can happen if the Apple iPhone 14 Pro Max device is in Airplane mode or if the user has turned off location sharing for the Find My app. It can also happen if there is a temporary issue with the iCloud servers.

In summary, "No Location Found" means the app could not locate the Apple iPhone 14 Pro Max device, while "Location Not Available" means that the Apple iPhone 14 Pro Max device is not currently sharing its location.
